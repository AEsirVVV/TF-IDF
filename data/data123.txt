Field Notes: Cider Making
A friend suggests handling carbonation a day earlier for a more even result. The argument about yeast never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Reviewing my notes, gravity and orchard need different handling in cold weather.
The toolkit can stay small; sweetness and bottling are enough if the technique is right. Halfway through I realised orchard is easier to overlook than gravity. Conclusion first: carbonation deserves more attention than barrel at this stage.
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep sweetness slow and gravity even.
