Canoe Building Notes
Halfway through I realised stem is easier to overlook than canvas. When progress stalls, revisit rib; that step is usually the culprit. Reorganising the bench: thwart and clamp run out fastest. With limited time I would firm up rib first and leave canvas for later. In practice keel decides the outcome, while gunwale quietly shapes sheer. Another note: keel varies far more than rib in this batch.
Canoe Building rewards patience: hold stem steady and the rest follows.
