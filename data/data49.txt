Notes on Bookbinding
The toolkit can stay small; awl and stitching are enough if the technique is right. Conclusion first: press deserves more attention than casing at this stage. Bookbinding rewards patience: hold headband steady and the rest follows. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from awl and glue.
Halfway through I realised glue is easier to overlook than awl. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough press, or far too much headband.
Seasonal shifts affect stitching more than beginners expect.
