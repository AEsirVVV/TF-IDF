Working Notes on Glassblowing
With limited time I would firm up kiln first and leave furnace for later. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from twist and blowpipe. Reorganising the bench: blowpipe and furnace run out fastest.
Glassblowing rewards patience: hold color steady and the rest follows.
If two runs disappoint, swap punty before touching twist. Conclusion first: twist deserves more attention than kiln at this stage.
