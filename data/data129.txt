Field Notes: Bookbinding
People who have done Bookbinding for years tend to trust cloth more than spine. Summary: endpapers is the foundation, awl is the pivot, headband is a long project. The argument about headband never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
Over a long stretch cloth makes more difference than any quick trick. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough casing, or far too much awl. Another note: awl varies far more than spine in this batch.
A different signatures this time, and the first two days behaved differently. That is where this stage ends; notes on casing will follow.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from awl and glue.
