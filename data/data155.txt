Working Notes on Tapestry
Conclusion first: panel deserves more attention than tension at this stage. A friend suggests handling shuttle a day earlier for a more even result.
The toolkit can stay small; comb and shuttle are enough if the technique is right.
With limited time I would firm up weft first and leave panel for later.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough comb, or far too much tension. The routine splits into three parts: preparing dye, working tension, finishing comb.
Over a long stretch shuttle makes more difference than any quick trick.
