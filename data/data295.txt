Notes on Archery
Conclusion first: bowstring deserves more attention than draw at this stage. Archery rewards patience: hold bowstring steady and the rest follows.
People who have done Archery for years tend to trust anchor more than arrow. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough draw, or far too much target. If two runs disappoint, swap quiver before touching draw. The argument about draw never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
