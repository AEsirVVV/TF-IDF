Notes on Sourdough Baking
A friend suggests handling hydration a day earlier for a more even result. With limited time I would firm up scoring first and leave crumb for later.
Beginners overlook starter and then struggle with loaf again and again. Summary: starter is the foundation, crumb is the pivot, oven is a long project. Another note: starter varies far more than oven in this batch. Seasonal shifts affect crust more than beginners expect.
If two runs disappoint, swap ferment before touching starter.
