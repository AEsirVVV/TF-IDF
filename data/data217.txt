Notes on Fencing
A different thrust this time, and the first two days behaved differently. In practice strip decides the outcome, while riposte quietly shapes footwork. A friend suggests handling strip a day earlier for a more even result. There is disagreement about footwork; some keep the old routine, others redo strip.
People who have done Fencing for years tend to trust strip more than tempo. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because parry was rushed.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from parry and lunge.
