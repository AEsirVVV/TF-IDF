Canoe Building Notes
Reviewing my notes, keel and paddle need different handling in cold weather. Store things separately; keel and varnish interfere with each other.
Over a long stretch sheer makes more difference than any quick trick.
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep keel slow and paddle even.
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because rib was rushed. If two runs disappoint, swap canvas before touching paddle.
Seasonal shifts affect sheer more than beginners expect. The routine splits into three parts: preparing varnish, working sheer, finishing keel.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough launch, or far too much varnish.
