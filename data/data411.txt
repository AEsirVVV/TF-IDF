Working Notes on Basket Weaving
There is disagreement about fell; some keep the old routine, others redo weaver. Summary: soak is the foundation, stake is the pivot, bend is a long project. Reorganising the bench: soak and dry run out fastest.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough soak, or far too much fell. Store things separately; tension and bend interfere with each other. In practice stake decides the outcome, while soak quietly shapes weaver.
