Working Notes on Slate Roofing
Halfway through I realised gauge is easier to overlook than weathering. Beginners overlook batten and then struggle with flashing again and again. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because flashing was rushed. Seasonal shifts affect flashing more than beginners expect. People who have done Slate Roofing for years tend to trust ridge more than weathering. Another note: gauge varies far more than lap in this batch.
