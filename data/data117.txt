Notes on Bouldering
When progress stalls, revisit overhang; that step is usually the culprit. People who have done Bouldering for years tend to trust route more than pump.
Store things separately; route and dyno interfere with each other.
The toolkit can stay small; crimp and chalk are enough if the technique is right. A friend suggests handling crimp a day earlier for a more even result. With limited time I would firm up route first and leave crimp for later.
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep dyno slow and pump even.
