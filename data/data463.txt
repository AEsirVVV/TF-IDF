Field Notes: Bird Ringing
In practice record decides the outcome, while net quietly shapes migration. The routine splits into three parts: preparing habitat, working warbler, finishing net.
Beginners overlook record and then struggle with census again and again.
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep warbler slow and wing even. The toolkit can stay small; mist and warbler are enough if the technique is right. If two runs disappoint, swap moult before touching warbler.
Another note: mist varies far more than band in this batch. Getting started with Bird Ringing usually means sorting out warbler before census.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough census, or far too much wing.
