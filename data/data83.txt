Organ Building Notes
Store things separately; reed and swell interfere with each other. Reviewing my notes, swell and pipe need different handling in cold weather.
With limited time I would firm up pipe first and leave bellows for later.
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because pipe was rushed. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from pipe and console. If two runs disappoint, swap console before touching swell.
Conclusion first: console deserves more attention than reed at this stage.
