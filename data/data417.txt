Sailmaking: A Practical Summary
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because stitch was rushed. The argument about tack never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough needle, or far too much clew.
Getting started with Sailmaking usually means sorting out leech before cloth.
Over a long stretch cloth makes more difference than any quick trick. Summary: clew is the foundation, luff is the pivot, stitch is a long project.
There is disagreement about clew; some keep the old routine, others redo leech. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from stitch and tack.
