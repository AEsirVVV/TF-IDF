Notes on Falconry
The toolkit can stay small; glove and quarry are enough if the technique is right. There is disagreement about hood; some keep the old routine, others redo lure.
Falconry rewards patience: hold lure steady and the rest follows. If two runs disappoint, swap lure before touching hood.
People who have done Falconry for years tend to trust glove more than lure. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough quarry, or far too much glove.
With limited time I would firm up glove first and leave hood for later.
