Working Notes on Letterpress
People who have done Letterpress for years tend to trust ink more than proof. Store things separately; paper and leading interfere with each other. Summary: quoin is the foundation, ink is the pivot, type is a long project. With limited time I would firm up ink first and leave quoin for later. A different registration this time, and the first two days behaved differently. Over a long stretch registration makes more difference than any quick trick.
