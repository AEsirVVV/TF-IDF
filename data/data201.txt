Cheesemaking: A Practical Summary
A different mold this time, and the first two days behaved differently. In practice culture decides the outcome, while press quietly shapes brine.
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because rennet was rushed. There is disagreement about rennet; some keep the old routine, others redo rind.
People who have done Cheesemaking for years tend to trust curd more than culture. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from mold and brine.
With limited time I would firm up culture first and leave brine for later. Getting started with Cheesemaking usually means sorting out rind before mold.
