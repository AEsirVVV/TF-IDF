Field Notes: Organ Building
With limited time I would firm up console first and leave reed for later. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from voicing and swell. Seasonal shifts affect swell more than beginners expect. A different stop this time, and the first two days behaved differently. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep pipe slow and reed even. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because tuning was rushed.
