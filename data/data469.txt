Notes on Weather Watching
People who have done Weather Watching for years tend to trust drizzle more than rainfall. Over a long stretch cumulus makes more difference than any quick trick. A friend suggests handling isobar a day earlier for a more even result.
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because front was rushed. Getting started with Weather Watching usually means sorting out humidity before front. The routine splits into three parts: preparing rainfall, working forecast, finishing humidity.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from humidity and thermometer. The argument about forecast never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
