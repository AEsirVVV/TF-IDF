Notes on Tapestry
Reorganising the bench: selvage and knot run out fastest. When progress stalls, revisit selvage; that step is usually the culprit.
With limited time I would firm up thread first and leave comb for later. Over a long stretch panel makes more difference than any quick trick.
Another note: thread varies far more than comb in this batch. Beginners overlook warp and then struggle with selvage again and again.
Seasonal shifts affect warp more than beginners expect. Halfway through I realised selvage is easier to overlook than thread. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough motif, or far too much comb. Reviewing my notes, shuttle and warp need different handling in cold weather.
