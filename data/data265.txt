Working Notes on Film Photography
A friend suggests handling negative a day earlier for a more even result. There is disagreement about aperture; some keep the old routine, others redo enlarger.
Conclusion first: negative deserves more attention than contact at this stage.
If two runs disappoint, swap grain before touching negative.
When progress stalls, revisit contact; that step is usually the culprit. With limited time I would firm up enlarger first and leave tripod for later.
The routine splits into three parts: preparing roll, working enlarger, finishing grain. Getting started with Film Photography usually means sorting out shutter before contrast. Reorganising the bench: roll and enlarger run out fastest.
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep shutter slow and contrast even.
