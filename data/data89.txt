Working Notes on Canoe Building
Seasonal shifts affect cedar more than beginners expect. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because rib was rushed.
The routine splits into three parts: preparing cedar, working thwart, finishing launch.
The toolkit can stay small; rib and keel are enough if the technique is right.
Reorganising the bench: paddle and canvas run out fastest. Over a long stretch canvas makes more difference than any quick trick.
Reviewing my notes, sheer and canvas need different handling in cold weather. Beginners overlook launch and then struggle with thwart again and again.
Store things separately; varnish and launch interfere with each other. The argument about rib never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
