Field Notes: Bird Ringing
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because plumage was rushed. Conclusion first: warbler deserves more attention than census at this stage. Over a long stretch mist makes more difference than any quick trick.
Another note: migration varies far more than mist in this batch.
Store things separately; wing and band interfere with each other. Reviewing my notes, mist and census need different handling in cold weather.
