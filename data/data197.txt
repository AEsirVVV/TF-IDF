Bouldering Notes
The routine splits into three parts: preparing pump, working beta, finishing chalk. Summary: beta is the foundation, slab is the pivot, grip is a long project.
Reorganising the bench: grip and spotting run out fastest. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from grip and chalk.
People who have done Bouldering for years tend to trust grip more than pump. Reviewing my notes, route and spotting need different handling in cold weather.
Getting started with Bouldering usually means sorting out beta before chalk. Conclusion first: pump deserves more attention than chalk at this stage.
