Basketball has evolved from a simple game invented by James Naismith in 1891 to a global sport worth billions. The NBA leads the professional scene, with stars like LeBron James and Stephen Curry captivating audiences worldwide. Modern basketball emphasizes three-point shooting, fast breaks, and positionless lineups. Analytics have transformed coaching strategies, with teams using data to optimize shot selection. The sport continues to grow, with international players making significant impacts and the Olympics showcasing the best talents.

Basketball is also a cultural phenomenon: street courts appear in cities from New York to Manila, and sneakers have become fashion icons. The Women's National Basketball Association continues to break attendance records, proving that great competition attracts audiences regardless of gender. Youth programs teach teamwork and discipline through the sport, while social media brings highlights to millions of fans instantly. From playgrounds to professional arenas, basketball remains a game of skill, speed, and heart.
