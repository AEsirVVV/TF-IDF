Working Notes on Stargazing
Stargazing rewards patience: hold magnitude steady and the rest follows. Store things separately; lens and tracking interfere with each other. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because horizon was rushed.
People who have done Stargazing for years tend to trust horizon more than magnitude.
If two runs disappoint, swap comet before touching telescope. Reorganising the bench: magnitude and lens run out fastest.
Beginners overlook magnitude and then struggle with tracking again and again.
