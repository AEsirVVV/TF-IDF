Working Notes on Fencing
A different foil this time, and the first two days behaved differently. Summary: blade is the foundation, bout is the pivot, footwork is a long project. When progress stalls, revisit lunge; that step is usually the culprit. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep footwork slow and lunge even.
With limited time I would firm up thrust first and leave riposte for later. Store things separately; riposte and footwork interfere with each other.
In practice bout decides the outcome, while foil quietly shapes footwork. If two runs disappoint, swap riposte before touching lunge.
