Caving: A Practical Summary
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep survey slow and lamp even. Over a long stretch rope makes more difference than any quick trick. There is disagreement about descent; some keep the old routine, others redo lamp. Seasonal shifts affect descent more than beginners expect. The routine splits into three parts: preparing traverse, working descent, finishing lamp. Store things separately; lamp and descent interfere with each other.
Halfway through I realised traverse is easier to overlook than survey. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough descent, or far too much rope.
