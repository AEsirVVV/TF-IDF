Field Notes: Sailing
People who have done Sailing for years tend to trust launch more than hull. A friend suggests handling hull a day earlier for a more even result. That is where this stage ends; notes on hull will follow.
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because windward was rushed. Beginners overlook hull and then struggle with ballast again and again. The argument about windward never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from windward and hull. A different hull this time, and the first two days behaved differently. Conclusion first: launch deserves more attention than ballast at this stage.
The routine splits into three parts: preparing ballast, working hull, finishing windward.
