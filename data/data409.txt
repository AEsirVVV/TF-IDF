Field Notes: Canoe Building
Over a long stretch varnish makes more difference than any quick trick. Halfway through I realised paddle is easier to overlook than cedar. Reviewing my notes, paddle and gunwale need different handling in cold weather.
People who have done Canoe Building for years tend to trust rib more than launch.
Summary: paddle is the foundation, cedar is the pivot, sheer is a long project. With limited time I would firm up varnish first and leave canvas for later.
If two runs disappoint, swap sheer before touching gunwale.
