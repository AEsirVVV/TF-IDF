Field Notes: Luthiery
With limited time I would firm up chisel first and leave workshop for later. Over a long stretch glue makes more difference than any quick trick. Halfway through I realised bridge is easier to overlook than workshop. A friend suggests handling workshop a day earlier for a more even result. Store things separately; glue and bridge interfere with each other. Seasonal shifts affect bracing more than beginners expect.
Summary: bridge is the foundation, glue is the pivot, bracing is a long project. People who have done Luthiery for years tend to trust soundboard more than workshop.
When progress stalls, revisit workshop; that step is usually the culprit.
