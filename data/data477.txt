Glassblowing: A Practical Summary
Beginners overlook crack and then struggle with bubble again and again. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep blowpipe slow and punty even.
Summary: bubble is the foundation, gather is the pivot, mold is a long project. Another note: punty varies far more than blowpipe in this batch. People who have done Glassblowing for years tend to trust punty more than gather.
