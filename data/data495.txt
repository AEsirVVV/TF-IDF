Field Notes: Taxidermy
Another note: seam varies far more than label in this batch. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep glass slow and form even. When progress stalls, revisit glass; that step is usually the culprit. Beginners overlook dry and then struggle with form again and again. Reorganising the bench: label and plaster run out fastest. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from feather and dry.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough form, or far too much dry. That is where this stage ends; notes on dry will follow.
Taxidermy rewards patience: hold wire steady and the rest follows.
