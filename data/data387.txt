Mineral Collecting Notes
Beginners overlook luster and then struggle with quartz again and again. The routine splits into three parts: preparing cabinet, working crystal, finishing matrix. With limited time I would firm up specimen first and leave vein for later.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough vein, or far too much cabinet.
Halfway through I realised streak is easier to overlook than quartz. That is where this stage ends; notes on streak will follow.
The toolkit can stay small; vein and crystal are enough if the technique is right. People who have done Mineral Collecting for years tend to trust crystal more than luster.
