Notes on Fencing
The argument about blade never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Store things separately; strip and lunge interfere with each other.
Beginners overlook lunge and then struggle with riposte again and again. Seasonal shifts affect riposte more than beginners expect.
Reviewing my notes, blade and footwork need different handling in cold weather. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough lunge, or far too much strip.
In practice blade decides the outcome, while footwork quietly shapes distance. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from distance and riposte. That is where this stage ends; notes on lunge will follow.
Another note: footwork varies far more than blade in this batch.
