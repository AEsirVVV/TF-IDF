Notes on Restoration
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because humidity was rushed. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep humidity slow and cloth even. Seasonal shifts affect cloth more than beginners expect.
With limited time I would firm up mount first and leave retouch for later.
Reorganising the bench: retouch and mount run out fastest. If two runs disappoint, swap veneer before touching cloth.
When progress stalls, revisit cloth; that step is usually the culprit. A friend suggests handling retouch a day earlier for a more even result.
The routine splits into three parts: preparing veneer, working humidity, finishing adhesive.
The toolkit can stay small; adhesive and veneer are enough if the technique is right.
