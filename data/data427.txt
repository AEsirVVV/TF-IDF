Field Notes: Woodworking
Halfway through I realised tenon is easier to overlook than clamping. Getting started with Woodworking usually means sorting out varnish before finish. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because finish was rushed.
When progress stalls, revisit grain; that step is usually the culprit.
The toolkit can stay small; plane and mortise are enough if the technique is right. Woodworking rewards patience: hold varnish steady and the rest follows.
That is where this stage ends; notes on sanding will follow. Seasonal shifts affect grain more than beginners expect.
