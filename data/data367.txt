Working Notes on Letterpress
The argument about proof never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. People who have done Letterpress for years tend to trust paper more than impression.
A friend suggests handling ink a day earlier for a more even result. There is disagreement about registration; some keep the old routine, others redo proof. In practice registration decides the outcome, while impression quietly shapes ink. When progress stalls, revisit type; that step is usually the culprit.
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep paper slow and proof even. Seasonal shifts affect proof more than beginners expect. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough proof, or far too much registration.
