Field Notes: Falconry
The routine splits into three parts: preparing glove, working flight, finishing training. There is disagreement about perch; some keep the old routine, others redo hood. The argument about training never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough hood, or far too much flight.
Getting started with Falconry usually means sorting out glove before quarry. If two runs disappoint, swap lure before touching weight.
