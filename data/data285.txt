Working Notes on Pottery
The routine splits into three parts: preparing throwing, working bisque, finishing kiln. The toolkit can stay small; wheel and wedging are enough if the technique is right.
Pottery rewards patience: hold bisque steady and the rest follows.
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because wheel was rushed. Another note: clay varies far more than bisque in this batch. Reviewing my notes, kiln and wedging need different handling in cold weather.
If two runs disappoint, swap wedging before touching stoneware. People who have done Pottery for years tend to trust firing more than clay.
