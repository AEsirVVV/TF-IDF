Kayaking: A Practical Summary
The routine splits into three parts: preparing spray, working hull, finishing current. The toolkit can stay small; rescue and portage are enough if the technique is right. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from roll and current.
When progress stalls, revisit rescue; that step is usually the culprit.
Reorganising the bench: eddy and roll run out fastest. The argument about portage never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
Getting started with Kayaking usually means sorting out ferry before rescue. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep roll slow and current even.
