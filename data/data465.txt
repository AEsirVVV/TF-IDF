Field Notes: Tidepooling
Store things separately; zone and surge interfere with each other. In practice anemone decides the outcome, while urchin quietly shapes pool. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep zone slow and kelp even.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from surge and mussel. Reviewing my notes, surge and kelp need different handling in cold weather. A different exposure this time, and the first two days behaved differently.
Halfway through I realised exposure is easier to overlook than shell. That is where this stage ends; notes on kelp will follow.
