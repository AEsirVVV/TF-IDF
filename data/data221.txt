Caving Notes
Halfway through I realised helmet is easier to overlook than descent. There is disagreement about pitch; some keep the old routine, others redo lamp. Getting started with Caving usually means sorting out lamp before descent.
Seasonal shifts affect lamp more than beginners expect.
Caving rewards patience: hold descent steady and the rest follows. The toolkit can stay small; passage and helmet are enough if the technique is right.
Another note: descent varies far more than passage in this batch. Store things separately; pitch and descent interfere with each other.
With limited time I would firm up lamp first and leave pitch for later. When progress stalls, revisit helmet; that step is usually the culprit.
