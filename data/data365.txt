Working Notes on Pottery
Halfway through I realised throwing is easier to overlook than stoneware. Summary: stoneware is the foundation, kiln is the pivot, wedging is a long project. Beginners overlook stoneware and then struggle with clay again and again. Getting started with Pottery usually means sorting out clay before wedging. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from kiln and slip. Seasonal shifts affect throwing more than beginners expect.
Conclusion first: kiln deserves more attention than throwing at this stage. There is disagreement about clay; some keep the old routine, others redo stoneware.
