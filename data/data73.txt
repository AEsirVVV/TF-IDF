Luthiery Notes
If two runs disappoint, swap soundboard before touching neck. When progress stalls, revisit bridge; that step is usually the culprit.
Over a long stretch neck makes more difference than any quick trick.
With limited time I would firm up soundboard first and leave fret for later. Reorganising the bench: varnish and bridge run out fastest. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from fret and bridge.
Conclusion first: bracing deserves more attention than fret at this stage. Reviewing my notes, fret and bracing need different handling in cold weather.
