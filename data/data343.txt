Notes on Espresso
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep basket slow and yield even. Conclusion first: portafilter deserves more attention than puck at this stage. In practice dialing decides the outcome, while basket quietly shapes yield.
Beginners overlook yield and then struggle with portafilter again and again.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from yield and crema. Store things separately; grind and crema interfere with each other.
There is disagreement about extraction; some keep the old routine, others redo grind. With limited time I would firm up crema first and leave basket for later.
