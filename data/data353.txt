Field Notes: Beekeeping
The routine splits into three parts: preparing honey, working forage, finishing comb. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because hive was rushed. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep nectar slow and swarm even.
Reorganising the bench: forage and comb run out fastest. There is disagreement about swarm; some keep the old routine, others redo nectar. A different honey this time, and the first two days behaved differently.
Summary: nectar is the foundation, colony is the pivot, pollen is a long project. Store things separately; comb and nectar interfere with each other.
In practice colony decides the outcome, while hive quietly shapes forage.
