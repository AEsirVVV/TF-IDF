Working Notes on Cheesemaking
Summary: rennet is the foundation, whey is the pivot, brine is a long project. Getting started with Cheesemaking usually means sorting out wheel before brine.
With limited time I would firm up wheel first and leave culture for later.
Cheesemaking rewards patience: hold curd steady and the rest follows. There is disagreement about culture; some keep the old routine, others redo whey. The toolkit can stay small; culture and curd are enough if the technique is right.
A friend suggests handling curd a day earlier for a more even result.
