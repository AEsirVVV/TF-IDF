Notes on Archery
Getting started with Archery usually means sorting out draw before arrow. Over a long stretch fletching makes more difference than any quick trick.
The argument about draw never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Reviewing my notes, release and bowstring need different handling in cold weather. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from release and tiller. Conclusion first: quiver deserves more attention than grouping at this stage.
With limited time I would firm up bowstring first and leave nock for later. If two runs disappoint, swap fletching before touching quiver.
That is where this stage ends; notes on quiver will follow.
