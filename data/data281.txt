Working Notes on Cheesemaking
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough mold, or far too much wheel. The argument about firmness never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because curd was rushed. Getting started with Cheesemaking usually means sorting out mold before culture. Summary: wheel is the foundation, firmness is the pivot, aging is a long project. People who have done Cheesemaking for years tend to trust rennet more than firmness.
The toolkit can stay small; firmness and rind are enough if the technique is right. Reorganising the bench: whey and firmness run out fastest.
Reviewing my notes, rennet and firmness need different handling in cold weather.
