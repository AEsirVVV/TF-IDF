Notes on Letterpress
A different ink this time, and the first two days behaved differently. That is where this stage ends; notes on ink will follow. People who have done Letterpress for years tend to trust leading more than type.
When progress stalls, revisit chase; that step is usually the culprit. The routine splits into three parts: preparing ink, working leading, finishing chase. Getting started with Letterpress usually means sorting out quoin before type.
