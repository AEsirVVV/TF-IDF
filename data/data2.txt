Climate change is the defining challenge of our era. Rising global temperatures, melting ice caps, and extreme weather events are clear signals that urgent action is needed. The Paris Agreement aims to limit warming to well below 2 degrees Celsius. Countries are transitioning to renewable energy sources like solar and wind power. Individuals can also contribute by reducing waste and using public transportation. However, achieving net-zero emissions by 2050 requires unprecedented international cooperation and technological innovation.

Scientists warn that every fraction of a degree of warming matters for ecosystems, agriculture, and coastal cities. Electric vehicles are replacing gasoline cars, and energy-efficient buildings reduce demand. Carbon capture technology, still expensive, may become a critical tool in the coming decades. Young people around the world have joined climate strikes, demanding that leaders treat the crisis with the urgency it deserves.
