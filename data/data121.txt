Field Notes: Cheesemaking
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from mold and whey. Halfway through I realised cave is easier to overlook than whey. Store things separately; cave and curd interfere with each other. When progress stalls, revisit rind; that step is usually the culprit.
Beginners overlook curd and then struggle with rennet again and again. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep aging slow and wheel even.
Over a long stretch rind makes more difference than any quick trick. In practice aging decides the outcome, while wheel quietly shapes rind.
