Working Notes on Kayaking
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because stroke was rushed. The argument about portage never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
The toolkit can stay small; stroke and paddle are enough if the technique is right. A different cockpit this time, and the first two days behaved differently. Beginners overlook cockpit and then struggle with stroke again and again. People who have done Kayaking for years tend to trust stroke more than rescue.
Reviewing my notes, rescue and roll need different handling in cold weather.
