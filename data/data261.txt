Sourdough Baking Notes
The routine splits into three parts: preparing proofing, working scoring, finishing crumb. A friend suggests handling crumb a day earlier for a more even result. The argument about proofing never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Conclusion first: dough deserves more attention than crust at this stage. Sourdough Baking rewards patience: hold dough steady and the rest follows. Store things separately; dough and spring interfere with each other.
Summary: dough is the foundation, spring is the pivot, crust is a long project. When progress stalls, revisit dough; that step is usually the culprit.
