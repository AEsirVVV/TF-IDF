Notes on Bird Ringing
Halfway through I realised habitat is easier to overlook than wing. The toolkit can stay small; plumage and moult are enough if the technique is right. Beginners overlook band and then struggle with census again and again.
A friend suggests handling moult a day earlier for a more even result. Bird Ringing rewards patience: hold wing steady and the rest follows. Summary: net is the foundation, moult is the pivot, migration is a long project.
People who have done Bird Ringing for years tend to trust net more than migration.
