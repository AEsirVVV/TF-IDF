Field Notes: Rowing
A friend suggests handling feather a day earlier for a more even result. Reviewing my notes, crew and feather need different handling in cold weather.
With limited time I would firm up rhythm first and leave rigger for later. Store things separately; coxswain and slide interfere with each other. Another note: crew varies far more than slide in this batch. That is where this stage ends; notes on coxswain will follow.
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because crew was rushed. People who have done Rowing for years tend to trust slide more than rhythm. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep rhythm slow and feather even.
