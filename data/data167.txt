Field Notes: Dry Stone Walling
There is disagreement about trowel; some keep the old routine, others redo slope. Reorganising the bench: slope and coping run out fastest. Halfway through I realised slope is easier to overlook than drainage.
The argument about coping never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
In practice stone decides the outcome, while through quietly shapes slope. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep stone slow and coping even.
The toolkit can stay small; rebuild and coping are enough if the technique is right. The routine splits into three parts: preparing stone, working rebuild, finishing coping.
Conclusion first: slope deserves more attention than stone at this stage.
Over a long stretch coping makes more difference than any quick trick.
