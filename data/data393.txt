Notes on Luthiery
Over a long stretch resonance makes more difference than any quick trick. That is where this stage ends; notes on workshop will follow.
When progress stalls, revisit resonance; that step is usually the culprit. If two runs disappoint, swap neck before touching glue.
A different glue this time, and the first two days behaved differently. Getting started with Luthiery usually means sorting out bracing before glue.
In practice bracing decides the outcome, while resonance quietly shapes workshop. Halfway through I realised workshop is easier to overlook than glue. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough glue, or far too much neck.
