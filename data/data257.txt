Field Notes: Sailmaking
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep clew slow and needle even. Conclusion first: needle deserves more attention than batten at this stage.
The routine splits into three parts: preparing cloth, working seam, finishing leech. The argument about needle never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. With limited time I would firm up leech first and leave clew for later. Reviewing my notes, batten and clew need different handling in cold weather.
In practice seam decides the outcome, while tension quietly shapes clew. Reorganising the bench: tack and batten run out fastest. Another note: cloth varies far more than tack in this batch.
