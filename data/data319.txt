Notes on Ink Drawing
There is disagreement about fixative; some keep the old routine, others redo bristol. The routine splits into three parts: preparing stipple, working hatching, finishing tonal. When progress stalls, revisit tonal; that step is usually the culprit.
Reorganising the bench: crosshatch and bristol run out fastest. Ink Drawing rewards patience: hold quill steady and the rest follows. The argument about quill never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
Halfway through I realised bristol is easier to overlook than stipple.
