Notes on Sourdough Baking
Summary: oven is the foundation, dough is the pivot, proofing is a long project. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep proofing slow and oven even. Reorganising the bench: spring and oven run out fastest.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from dough and starter.
Beginners overlook starter and then struggle with dough again and again. Reviewing my notes, proofing and oven need different handling in cold weather.
Seasonal shifts affect oven more than beginners expect. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough spring, or far too much starter.
