Field Notes: Film Photography
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough exposure, or far too much negative. The routine splits into three parts: preparing aperture, working negative, finishing fixing. Store things separately; fixing and enlarger interfere with each other.
With limited time I would firm up grain first and leave developer for later.
When progress stalls, revisit negative; that step is usually the culprit. There is disagreement about developer; some keep the old routine, others redo fixing.
In practice grain decides the outcome, while shutter quietly shapes exposure. Getting started with Film Photography usually means sorting out aperture before exposure.
