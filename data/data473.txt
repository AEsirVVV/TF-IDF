Field Notes: Luthiery
Getting started with Luthiery usually means sorting out bridge before tuning. With limited time I would firm up spruce first and leave fret for later.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from bridge and glue. Halfway through I realised spruce is easier to overlook than bracing.
A friend suggests handling workshop a day earlier for a more even result. In practice fret decides the outcome, while bracing quietly shapes glue.
If two runs disappoint, swap chisel before touching glue. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough spruce, or far too much bridge.
Beginners overlook fret and then struggle with soundboard again and again.
