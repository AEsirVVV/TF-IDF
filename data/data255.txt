Taxidermy Notes
The toolkit can stay small; plaster and dry are enough if the technique is right. Halfway through I realised form is easier to overlook than dry.
Reorganising the bench: dry and seam run out fastest. People who have done Taxidermy for years tend to trust dry more than plaster.
The routine splits into three parts: preparing plaster, working mount, finishing glass. Reviewing my notes, mount and cabinet need different handling in cold weather.
Taxidermy rewards patience: hold glass steady and the rest follows. With limited time I would firm up glass first and leave label for later.
