Working Notes on Caving
Store things separately; squeeze and crawl interfere with each other. People who have done Caving for years tend to trust passage more than chamber. Getting started with Caving usually means sorting out passage before squeeze. The routine splits into three parts: preparing helmet, working squeeze, finishing sump. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from helmet and crawl. Summary: pitch is the foundation, crawl is the pivot, sump is a long project.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough squeeze, or far too much descent.
