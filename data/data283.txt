Cider Making: A Practical Summary
Over a long stretch apples makes more difference than any quick trick. The routine splits into three parts: preparing yeast, working carbonation, finishing bottling.
Conclusion first: fermentation deserves more attention than barrel at this stage. Halfway through I realised barrel is easier to overlook than fermentation.
A friend suggests handling tannin a day earlier for a more even result. If two runs disappoint, swap barrel before touching gravity.
When progress stalls, revisit tannin; that step is usually the culprit.
