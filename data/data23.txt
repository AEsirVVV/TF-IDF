Espresso Notes
Over a long stretch roast makes more difference than any quick trick. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough grind, or far too much portafilter. Reorganising the bench: grind and portafilter run out fastest. Reviewing my notes, extraction and bitterness need different handling in cold weather.
Summary: extraction is the foundation, grind is the pivot, pressure is a long project. Getting started with Espresso usually means sorting out basket before portafilter.
If two runs disappoint, swap bitterness before touching extraction.
