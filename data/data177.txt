Sailmaking: A Practical Summary
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough stitch, or far too much luff. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from batten and palm. The routine splits into three parts: preparing batten, working luff, finishing tension.
Another note: stitch varies far more than palm in this batch.
Reviewing my notes, tension and seam need different handling in cold weather. Beginners overlook boltrope and then struggle with cloth again and again.
Sailmaking rewards patience: hold palm steady and the rest follows. People who have done Sailmaking for years tend to trust stitch more than tension.
