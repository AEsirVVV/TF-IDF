Rowing Notes
A different oar this time, and the first two days behaved differently. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because rigger was rushed.
Seasonal shifts affect slide more than beginners expect. The toolkit can stay small; regatta and oar are enough if the technique is right. When progress stalls, revisit rigger; that step is usually the culprit. Summary: slide is the foundation, regatta is the pivot, shell is a long project.
Rowing rewards patience: hold oar steady and the rest follows. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough rigger, or far too much crew.
