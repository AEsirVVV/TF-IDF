Working Notes on Glassblowing
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough color, or far too much mold. Glassblowing rewards patience: hold furnace steady and the rest follows.
The routine splits into three parts: preparing mold, working gather, finishing blowpipe.
Store things separately; mold and twist interfere with each other.
Getting started with Glassblowing usually means sorting out color before furnace. Reorganising the bench: twist and blowpipe run out fastest.
