Sourdough Baking Notes
A different levain this time, and the first two days behaved differently. Another note: starter varies far more than crumb in this batch. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough ferment, or far too much starter. Beginners overlook proofing and then struggle with crumb again and again.
Seasonal shifts affect crumb more than beginners expect. There is disagreement about ferment; some keep the old routine, others redo proofing.
Store things separately; ferment and levain interfere with each other.
