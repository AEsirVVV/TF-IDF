Field Notes: Slate Roofing
Reviewing my notes, eaves and ridge need different handling in cold weather. Seasonal shifts affect flashing more than beginners expect. Halfway through I realised gauge is easier to overlook than flashing.
That is where this stage ends; notes on flashing will follow.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough ridge, or far too much batten. The argument about eaves never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
