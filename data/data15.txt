Space exploration represents humanity's greatest adventure. Since the first satellite was launched in 1957, we have sent robots to Mars, landed humans on the Moon, and placed powerful telescopes in orbit that peer billions of light-years into the past. The International Space Station hosts astronauts from many nations conducting experiments in zero gravity, from growing plants to studying how the human body adapts.

Private companies have dramatically lowered the cost of launching rockets, opening space to commercial satellites, tourism, and future missions to the Moon and Mars. Scientists search for signs of life on icy moons like Europa and examine exoplanets for atmospheres that might support living organisms. Space technology also benefits life on Earth, improving weather forecasting, global communications, and navigation. The next great leap may be a permanent human presence beyond our planet.
