Notes on Falconry
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep flight slow and perch even. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough glove, or far too much perch.
In practice glove decides the outcome, while flight quietly shapes quarry. There is disagreement about hood; some keep the old routine, others redo quarry. When progress stalls, revisit weight; that step is usually the culprit. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because flight was rushed.
Getting started with Falconry usually means sorting out quarry before glove. Another note: perch varies far more than quarry in this batch.
