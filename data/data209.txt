Bookbinding Notes
Beginners overlook endpapers and then struggle with cloth again and again. Reorganising the bench: press and glue run out fastest. Reviewing my notes, endpapers and press need different handling in cold weather.
There is disagreement about cloth; some keep the old routine, others redo signatures. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from endpapers and repair. Seasonal shifts affect endpapers more than beginners expect.
