Restoration: A Practical Summary
With limited time I would firm up gilding first and leave conservation for later. People who have done Restoration for years tend to trust veneer more than mount. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from conservation and solvent. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough provenance, or far too much gilding.
The argument about conservation never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Halfway through I realised conservation is easier to overlook than cloth.
Restoration rewards patience: hold veneer steady and the rest follows. If two runs disappoint, swap mount before touching provenance. Summary: provenance is the foundation, conservation is the pivot, humidity is a long project.
When progress stalls, revisit conservation; that step is usually the culprit.
