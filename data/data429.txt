Field Notes: Sailing
Store things separately; dinghy and ballast interfere with each other. The routine splits into three parts: preparing rigging, working rudder, finishing ballast.
Halfway through I realised launch is easier to overlook than dinghy. Seasonal shifts affect ballast more than beginners expect. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because keel was rushed. People who have done Sailing for years tend to trust tacking more than rudder.
