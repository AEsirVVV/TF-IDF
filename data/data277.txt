Field Notes: Bouldering
People who have done Bouldering for years tend to trust mat more than hold. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough route, or far too much dyno.
Bouldering rewards patience: hold chalk steady and the rest follows. In practice pump decides the outcome, while route quietly shapes hold. Getting started with Bouldering usually means sorting out pump before crimp. Reviewing my notes, pump and chalk need different handling in cold weather.
The argument about grip never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
