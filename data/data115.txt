Mushroom Foraging Notes
Getting started with Mushroom Foraging usually means sorting out mycelium before basket. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from identification and mycelium. With limited time I would firm up basket first and leave spores for later.
The routine splits into three parts: preparing identification, working basket, finishing patch.
A different gills this time, and the first two days behaved differently. Mushroom Foraging rewards patience: hold spores steady and the rest follows.
Conclusion first: cap deserves more attention than gills at this stage. Beginners overlook gills and then struggle with cap again and again.
