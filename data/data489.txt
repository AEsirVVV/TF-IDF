Working Notes on Canoe Building
Beginners overlook paddle and then struggle with varnish again and again. Store things separately; paddle and keel interfere with each other.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from stem and rib.
Getting started with Canoe Building usually means sorting out stem before keel. In practice rib decides the outcome, while sheer quietly shapes paddle. Reorganising the bench: launch and rib run out fastest.
When progress stalls, revisit launch; that step is usually the culprit. The routine splits into three parts: preparing paddle, working keel, finishing stem. People who have done Canoe Building for years tend to trust gunwale more than sheer.
