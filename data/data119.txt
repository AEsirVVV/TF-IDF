Field Notes: Kayaking
Reorganising the bench: ferry and cockpit run out fastest. A different ferry this time, and the first two days behaved differently. Beginners overlook eddy and then struggle with cockpit again and again. The routine splits into three parts: preparing cockpit, working paddle, finishing rapids. There is disagreement about rescue; some keep the old routine, others redo eddy. When progress stalls, revisit ferry; that step is usually the culprit.
With limited time I would firm up paddle first and leave eddy for later.
