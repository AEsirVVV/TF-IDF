Working Notes on Espresso
Espresso rewards patience: hold bitterness steady and the rest follows. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from pressure and puck. Halfway through I realised pressure is easier to overlook than crema. Beginners overlook portafilter and then struggle with dialing again and again. The argument about extraction never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Reviewing my notes, portafilter and grind need different handling in cold weather.
That is where this stage ends; notes on puck will follow. If two runs disappoint, swap puck before touching bitterness.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough pressure, or far too much yield.
