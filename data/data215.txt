Notes on Archery
Another note: draw varies far more than fletching in this batch. Over a long stretch spine makes more difference than any quick trick.
The routine splits into three parts: preparing release, working fletching, finishing arrow.
Archery rewards patience: hold arrow steady and the rest follows.
People who have done Archery for years tend to trust spine more than target. Getting started with Archery usually means sorting out draw before fletching.
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep fletching slow and release even.
