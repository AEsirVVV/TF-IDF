Working Notes on Rowing
With limited time I would firm up oar first and leave shell for later. When progress stalls, revisit feather; that step is usually the culprit.
The argument about crew never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. In practice feather decides the outcome, while oar quietly shapes rhythm. Summary: catch is the foundation, coxswain is the pivot, oar is a long project. The toolkit can stay small; feather and stroke are enough if the technique is right.
Beginners overlook regatta and then struggle with rigger again and again. Seasonal shifts affect oar more than beginners expect.
