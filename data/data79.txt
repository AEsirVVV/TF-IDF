Ink Drawing Notes
A friend suggests handling crosshatch a day earlier for a more even result. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep gesture slow and contour even. If two runs disappoint, swap sketchbook before touching hatching. Ink Drawing rewards patience: hold fixative steady and the rest follows. That is where this stage ends; notes on hatching will follow. Store things separately; contour and hatching interfere with each other.
The routine splits into three parts: preparing sketchbook, working crosshatch, finishing contour. The toolkit can stay small; sketchbook and crosshatch are enough if the technique is right. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough sketchbook, or far too much gesture.
