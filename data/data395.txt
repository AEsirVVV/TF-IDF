Field Notes: Tapestry
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from shuttle and warp. Reorganising the bench: warp and shuttle run out fastest.
The argument about shuttle never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Beginners overlook selvage and then struggle with warp again and again.
In practice loom decides the outcome, while selvage quietly shapes warp. Summary: loom is the foundation, panel is the pivot, dye is a long project.
Tapestry rewards patience: hold dye steady and the rest follows. A friend suggests handling motif a day earlier for a more even result.
When progress stalls, revisit warp; that step is usually the culprit.
With limited time I would firm up selvage first and leave motif for later.
