Watchmaking Notes
Beginners overlook hands and then struggle with polishing again and again. Summary: movement is the foundation, winding is the pivot, dial is a long project.
Conclusion first: winding deserves more attention than hands at this stage.
With limited time I would firm up hands first and leave polishing for later. In practice winding decides the outcome, while hands quietly shapes polishing. Watchmaking rewards patience: hold spring steady and the rest follows.
Another note: winding varies far more than polishing in this batch. A friend suggests handling spring a day earlier for a more even result. Getting started with Watchmaking usually means sorting out movement before hands.
