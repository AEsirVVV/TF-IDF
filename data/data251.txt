Basket Weaving Notes
The toolkit can stay small; dry and fell are enough if the technique is right. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from sides and willow.
People who have done Basket Weaving for years tend to trust tension more than willow.
Basket Weaving rewards patience: hold dry steady and the rest follows.
Store things separately; stake and tension interfere with each other. The routine splits into three parts: preparing weaver, working dry, finishing base.
