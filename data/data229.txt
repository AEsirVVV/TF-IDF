Notes on Weather Watching
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough humidity, or far too much front. Conclusion first: barometer deserves more attention than thunder at this stage. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep humidity slow and gust even. With limited time I would firm up cumulus first and leave gust for later. The toolkit can stay small; isobar and barometer are enough if the technique is right. In practice rainfall decides the outcome, while cumulus quietly shapes gust.
A friend suggests handling rainfall a day earlier for a more even result. Reorganising the bench: dew and barometer run out fastest. The argument about drizzle never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
