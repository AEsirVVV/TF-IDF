Field Notes: Ink Drawing
Store things separately; hatching and nib interfere with each other. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because fixative was rushed.
Beginners overlook sketchbook and then struggle with fixative again and again. Ink Drawing rewards patience: hold crosshatch steady and the rest follows.
With limited time I would firm up crosshatch first and leave sketchbook for later. Conclusion first: fixative deserves more attention than nib at this stage.
The toolkit can stay small; gesture and fixative are enough if the technique is right. There is disagreement about crosshatch; some keep the old routine, others redo nib.
