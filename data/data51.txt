Watchmaking: A Practical Summary
Beginners overlook balance and then struggle with case again and again. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough movement, or far too much gear. Reviewing my notes, gear and hands need different handling in cold weather. People who have done Watchmaking for years tend to trust case more than dial.
Reorganising the bench: movement and hands run out fastest. Getting started with Watchmaking usually means sorting out calibration before case.
The argument about gear never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
