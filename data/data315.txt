Tapestry Notes
Conclusion first: thread deserves more attention than knot at this stage. Reorganising the bench: shuttle and tension run out fastest.
If two runs disappoint, swap comb before touching selvage. Another note: tension varies far more than selvage in this batch.
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep knot slow and thread even. Beginners overlook dye and then struggle with knot again and again.
With limited time I would firm up comb first and leave selvage for later.
