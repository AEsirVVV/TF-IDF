Rowing: A Practical Summary
Getting started with Rowing usually means sorting out rigger before feather. Conclusion first: oar deserves more attention than feather at this stage.
Halfway through I realised rigger is easier to overlook than slide. Another note: stroke varies far more than recovery in this batch. Seasonal shifts affect feather more than beginners expect. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from oar and feather.
There is disagreement about slide; some keep the old routine, others redo rigger. Rowing rewards patience: hold feather steady and the rest follows.
