Notes on Bird Ringing
With limited time I would firm up net first and leave moult for later. Store things separately; habitat and net interfere with each other. A different moult this time, and the first two days behaved differently. In practice habitat decides the outcome, while moult quietly shapes release. Bird Ringing rewards patience: hold net steady and the rest follows. The routine splits into three parts: preparing moult, working migration, finishing net.
