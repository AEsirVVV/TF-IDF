Notes on Woodworking
The toolkit can stay small; clamping and sanding are enough if the technique is right. When progress stalls, revisit grain; that step is usually the culprit.
Store things separately; varnish and sanding interfere with each other.
The routine splits into three parts: preparing sanding, working plane, finishing chisel. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough clamping, or far too much hardwood. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because clamping was rushed.
Conclusion first: varnish deserves more attention than hardwood at this stage. A friend suggests handling plane a day earlier for a more even result. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from varnish and chisel.
