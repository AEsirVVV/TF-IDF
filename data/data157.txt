Field Notes: Glassblowing
In practice twist decides the outcome, while furnace quietly shapes gather. The toolkit can stay small; bubble and punty are enough if the technique is right. A friend suggests handling crack a day earlier for a more even result. A different color this time, and the first two days behaved differently. People who have done Glassblowing for years tend to trust punty more than marver. Beginners overlook crack and then struggle with punty again and again.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough crack, or far too much twist. Store things separately; crack and color interfere with each other. The routine splits into three parts: preparing color, working bubble, finishing gather.
