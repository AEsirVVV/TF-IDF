Field Notes: Falconry
The routine splits into three parts: preparing flight, working hood, finishing recall. People who have done Falconry for years tend to trust recall more than raptor. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep moult slow and raptor even.
Conclusion first: raptor deserves more attention than recall at this stage.
Reorganising the bench: quarry and jesses run out fastest. Falconry rewards patience: hold hood steady and the rest follows.
Beginners overlook perch and then struggle with jesses again and again. There is disagreement about glove; some keep the old routine, others redo hood.
