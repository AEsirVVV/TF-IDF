Working Notes on Sailmaking
Beginners overlook batten and then struggle with seam again and again. With limited time I would firm up batten first and leave tension for later.
Another note: needle varies far more than luff in this batch. Halfway through I realised luff is easier to overlook than needle.
The routine splits into three parts: preparing stitch, working batten, finishing tack. People who have done Sailmaking for years tend to trust seam more than needle.
