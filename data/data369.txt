This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from glue and headband. Store things separately; repair and glue interfere with each other. When progress stalls, revisit repair; that step is usually the culprit.
Over a long stretch awl makes more difference than any quick trick.
Reviewing my notes, awl and boards need different handling in cold weather. That is where this stage ends; notes on boards will follow.
Reorganising the bench: boards and glue run out fastest. The toolkit can stay small; cloth and awl are enough if the technique is right.
