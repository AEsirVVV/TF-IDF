Field Notes: Watchmaking
Another note: gear varies far more than balance in this batch. The routine splits into three parts: preparing gear, working jewel, finishing movement. Watchmaking rewards patience: hold calibration steady and the rest follows.
Halfway through I realised movement is easier to overlook than jewel. In practice calibration decides the outcome, while jewel quietly shapes hands. With limited time I would firm up calibration first and leave gear for later.
Conclusion first: hands deserves more attention than balance at this stage. Reorganising the bench: hands and balance run out fastest.
Reviewing my notes, balance and movement need different handling in cold weather.
