Field Notes: Horology Tools
The routine splits into three parts: preparing burnisher, working broach, finishing collet. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep loupe slow and lathe even. People who have done Horology Tools for years tend to trust lathe more than collet.
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because pivot was rushed.
Getting started with Horology Tools usually means sorting out escapement before pivot. Horology Tools rewards patience: hold stake steady and the rest follows.
Reviewing my notes, burnisher and broach need different handling in cold weather. Halfway through I realised pivot is easier to overlook than lathe. If two runs disappoint, swap stake before touching loupe.
