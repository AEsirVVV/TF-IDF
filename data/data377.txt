Fencing Notes
Beginners overlook bout and then struggle with guard again and again. The routine splits into three parts: preparing footwork, working bout, finishing foil.
The argument about riposte never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep tempo slow and distance even.
Seasonal shifts affect tempo more than beginners expect. The toolkit can stay small; riposte and parry are enough if the technique is right.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough parry, or far too much bout. With limited time I would firm up bout first and leave foil for later.
