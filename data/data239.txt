Notes on Ink Drawing
Ink Drawing rewards patience: hold fixative steady and the rest follows. The argument about nib never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
Conclusion first: stipple deserves more attention than bristol at this stage.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from stipple and bristol.
Reviewing my notes, stipple and fixative need different handling in cold weather. Beginners overlook nib and then struggle with stipple again and again.
There is disagreement about bristol; some keep the old routine, others redo crosshatch. Seasonal shifts affect nib more than beginners expect.
