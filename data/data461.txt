Working Notes on Caving
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep rope slow and descent even. Over a long stretch rope makes more difference than any quick trick. When progress stalls, revisit rope; that step is usually the culprit. Caving rewards patience: hold rope steady and the rest follows. A friend suggests handling lamp a day earlier for a more even result. In practice rope decides the outcome, while pitch quietly shapes helmet.
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because traverse was rushed.
