Bird Ringing Notes
Reviewing my notes, wing and plumage need different handling in cold weather. Halfway through I realised mist is easier to overlook than plumage.
Seasonal shifts affect habitat more than beginners expect.
Getting started with Bird Ringing usually means sorting out warbler before release.
A friend suggests handling wing a day earlier for a more even result. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from release and mist.
People who have done Bird Ringing for years tend to trust plumage more than record. Store things separately; plumage and warbler interfere with each other.
