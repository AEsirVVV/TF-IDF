Notes on Cider Making
A different sweetness this time, and the first two days behaved differently. Beginners overlook pressing and then struggle with orchard again and again. Reviewing my notes, carbonation and fermentation need different handling in cold weather. The toolkit can stay small; barrel and bottling are enough if the technique is right. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep bottling slow and yeast even. Reorganising the bench: orchard and blend run out fastest.
