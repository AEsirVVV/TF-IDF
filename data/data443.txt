Notes on Cider Making
A friend suggests handling sweetness a day earlier for a more even result. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep yeast slow and orchard even.
Reviewing my notes, yeast and bottling need different handling in cold weather.
Cider Making rewards patience: hold sweetness steady and the rest follows.
If two runs disappoint, swap bottling before touching orchard. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because carbonation was rushed.
When progress stalls, revisit fermentation; that step is usually the culprit. Seasonal shifts affect sweetness more than beginners expect. Conclusion first: yeast deserves more attention than bottling at this stage.
