Working Notes on Glassblowing
Glassblowing rewards patience: hold color steady and the rest follows. Beginners overlook gather and then struggle with crack again and again.
The toolkit can stay small; kiln and punty are enough if the technique is right. That is where this stage ends; notes on kiln will follow. Halfway through I realised crack is easier to overlook than mold. Reorganising the bench: punty and color run out fastest.
A friend suggests handling color a day earlier for a more even result. With limited time I would firm up mold first and leave punty for later.
