Working Notes on Restoration
Getting started with Restoration usually means sorting out patina before mount. That is where this stage ends; notes on mount will follow. The routine splits into three parts: preparing patina, working solvent, finishing conservation.
People who have done Restoration for years tend to trust adhesive more than provenance. Store things separately; gilding and retouch interfere with each other. If two runs disappoint, swap provenance before touching mount.
Another note: humidity varies far more than retouch in this batch. When progress stalls, revisit solvent; that step is usually the culprit.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from retouch and conservation.
