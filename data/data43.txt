Cider Making: A Practical Summary
Beginners overlook sweetness and then struggle with tannin again and again. With limited time I would firm up tannin first and leave blend for later. That is where this stage ends; notes on barrel will follow.
Store things separately; blend and barrel interfere with each other. People who have done Cider Making for years tend to trust pressing more than blend. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep sweetness slow and carbonation even.
In practice pressing decides the outcome, while barrel quietly shapes orchard.
