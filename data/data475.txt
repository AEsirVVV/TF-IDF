Tapestry: A Practical Summary
A different weft this time, and the first two days behaved differently. Over a long stretch weft makes more difference than any quick trick. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep knot slow and warp even. That is where this stage ends; notes on warp will follow.
Summary: warp is the foundation, dye is the pivot, shuttle is a long project. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough knot, or far too much dye.
In practice shuttle decides the outcome, while weft quietly shapes warp. Getting started with Tapestry usually means sorting out comb before weft. Store things separately; shuttle and knot interfere with each other.
