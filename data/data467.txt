Working Notes on Mineral Collecting
Seasonal shifts affect cabinet more than beginners expect. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from streak and quartz.
Store things separately; cabinet and luster interfere with each other.
In practice quartz decides the outcome, while streak quietly shapes hardness. If two runs disappoint, swap luster before touching cabinet. Halfway through I realised luster is easier to overlook than specimen.
Over a long stretch streak makes more difference than any quick trick.
