Working Notes on Restoration
Conclusion first: patina deserves more attention than nitrate at this stage. The toolkit can stay small; patina and provenance are enough if the technique is right.
Over a long stretch nitrate makes more difference than any quick trick. Another note: provenance varies far more than retouch in this batch. Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because patina was rushed. Beginners overlook gilding and then struggle with nitrate again and again.
If two runs disappoint, swap adhesive before touching mount.
