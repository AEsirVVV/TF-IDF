Woodworking: A Practical Summary
Reviewing my notes, clamping and tenon need different handling in cold weather. Beginners overlook tenon and then struggle with hardwood again and again. Reorganising the bench: grain and plane run out fastest.
Woodworking rewards patience: hold sanding steady and the rest follows. That is where this stage ends; notes on joinery will follow. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep sanding slow and plane even.
