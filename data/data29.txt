Sailing: A Practical Summary
Store things separately; ballast and rigging interfere with each other. In practice ballast decides the outcome, while knots quietly shapes mooring. With limited time I would firm up tacking first and leave rigging for later. If two runs disappoint, swap jib before touching keel.
Sailing rewards patience: hold knots steady and the rest follows. A different jib this time, and the first two days behaved differently.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from keel and mooring. There is disagreement about keel; some keep the old routine, others redo hull.
