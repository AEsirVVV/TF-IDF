Beekeeping: A Practical Summary
Getting started with Beekeeping usually means sorting out comb before queen. The argument about queen never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Beekeeping rewards patience: hold brood steady and the rest follows. Reviewing my notes, nectar and brood need different handling in cold weather. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep forage slow and swarm even. With limited time I would firm up brood first and leave smoker for later.
Conclusion first: swarm deserves more attention than forage at this stage.
