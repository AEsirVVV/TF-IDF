Field Notes: Fencing
The toolkit can stay small; parry and tempo are enough if the technique is right. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from riposte and lunge.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough footwork, or far too much riposte. A friend suggests handling riposte a day earlier for a more even result. The argument about riposte never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. With limited time I would firm up strip first and leave riposte for later.
Beginners overlook footwork and then struggle with tempo again and again. People who have done Fencing for years tend to trust lunge more than strip. Conclusion first: blade deserves more attention than tempo at this stage.
