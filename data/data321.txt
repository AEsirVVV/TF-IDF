Restoration: A Practical Summary
Seasonal shifts affect cloth more than beginners expect. Restoration rewards patience: hold gilding steady and the rest follows. Beginners overlook cloth and then struggle with adhesive again and again.
Over a long stretch cloth makes more difference than any quick trick. People who have done Restoration for years tend to trust mount more than veneer. Conclusion first: veneer deserves more attention than conservation at this stage.
Store things separately; retouch and cloth interfere with each other. Halfway through I realised gilding is easier to overlook than retouch. Another note: gilding varies far more than mount in this batch.
