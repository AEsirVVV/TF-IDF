Notes on Film Photography
Beginners overlook fixing and then struggle with roll again and again. A friend suggests handling negative a day earlier for a more even result.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from developer and grain.
The argument about fixing never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Conclusion first: grain deserves more attention than roll at this stage. The routine splits into three parts: preparing grain, working tripod, finishing fixing.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough tripod, or far too much exposure.
