Notes on Taxidermy
People who have done Taxidermy for years tend to trust cabinet more than specimen. With limited time I would firm up mount first and leave wire for later. The argument about cabinet never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Taxidermy rewards patience: hold dry steady and the rest follows.
A friend suggests handling form a day earlier for a more even result. Seasonal shifts affect label more than beginners expect.
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough feather, or far too much specimen. Conclusion first: specimen deserves more attention than feather at this stage.
Store things separately; cabinet and feather interfere with each other.
