Field Notes: Taxidermy
If two runs disappoint, swap pose before touching label. Beginners overlook form and then struggle with pose again and again.
Seasonal shifts affect mount more than beginners expect. This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from plaster and form. With limited time I would firm up cabinet first and leave form for later. In practice feather decides the outcome, while pose quietly shapes label.
Another note: label varies far more than plaster in this batch. Store things separately; specimen and plaster interfere with each other.
