Field Notes: Espresso
Espresso rewards patience: hold crema steady and the rest follows. A different roast this time, and the first two days behaved differently. With limited time I would firm up puck first and leave portafilter for later.
Seasonal shifts affect extraction more than beginners expect.
The argument about extraction never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Another note: bitterness varies far more than roast in this batch.
If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep puck slow and portafilter even. Summary: puck is the foundation, portafilter is the pivot, bitterness is a long project.
