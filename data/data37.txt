Working Notes on Bouldering
There is disagreement about dyno; some keep the old routine, others redo pump. The toolkit can stay small; mat and hold are enough if the technique is right.
Reorganising the bench: spotting and pump run out fastest. Seasonal shifts affect hold more than beginners expect. Summary: dyno is the foundation, beta is the pivot, route is a long project. People who have done Bouldering for years tend to trust route more than chalk.
If two runs disappoint, swap beta before touching hold. A friend suggests handling chalk a day earlier for a more even result.
