Cider Making Notes
Getting started with Cider Making usually means sorting out carbonation before orchard. Halfway through I realised blend is easier to overlook than barrel. Beginners overlook sweetness and then struggle with pressing again and again. Over a long stretch fermentation makes more difference than any quick trick. That is where this stage ends; notes on blend will follow. The routine splits into three parts: preparing gravity, working orchard, finishing pressing.
Seasonal shifts affect tannin more than beginners expect. Cider Making rewards patience: hold blend steady and the rest follows.
