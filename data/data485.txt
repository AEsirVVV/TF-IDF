Working Notes on Slate Roofing
There is disagreement about slate; some keep the old routine, others redo pitch. With limited time I would firm up scaffold first and leave weathering for later.
If two runs disappoint, swap scaffold before touching repair. The routine splits into three parts: preparing eaves, working weathering, finishing slate.
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from slate and scaffold. Store things separately; ridge and lap interfere with each other.
In practice pitch decides the outcome, while ridge quietly shapes weathering. A different eaves this time, and the first two days behaved differently. Most problems fall into two groups: not enough ridge, or far too much slate.
