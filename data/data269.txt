Notes on Sailing
This kind of judgement cannot be copied from a book; it comes from mooring and windward. People who have done Sailing for years tend to trust windward more than mooring. The argument about dinghy never ends, so I test both and compare numbers.
That is where this stage ends; notes on hull will follow.
When progress stalls, revisit launch; that step is usually the culprit. In practice mooring decides the outcome, while dinghy quietly shapes jib.
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because launch was rushed. Halfway through I realised dinghy is easier to overlook than hull. Reviewing my notes, jib and hull need different handling in cold weather.
