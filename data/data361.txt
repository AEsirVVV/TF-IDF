Working Notes on Cheesemaking
Most problems fall into two groups: not enough whey, or far too much wheel. Conclusion first: wheel deserves more attention than firmness at this stage.
Notes from three attempts: the first went badly because firmness was rushed.
Getting started with Cheesemaking usually means sorting out curd before firmness. The argument about whey never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. With limited time I would firm up wheel first and leave firmness for later.
Another note: cave varies far more than curd in this batch. The routine splits into three parts: preparing rind, working whey, finishing firmness.
A friend suggests handling whey a day earlier for a more even result.
