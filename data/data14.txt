Economics studies how people, companies, and governments allocate scarce resources. Market economies rely on supply and demand to set prices, while central banks use interest rates and money supply to control inflation and encourage growth. Global trade connects nations and powers the world economy, allowing countries to specialize in what they produce best. The rise of digital payments, cryptocurrencies, and online marketplaces is transforming how money moves around the world.

Economic crises, from bank failures to recessions, show how quickly confidence can collapse when institutions are mismanaged. Governments respond with stimulus packages and regulation to protect consumers and stabilize markets. Economists use data on unemployment, GDP, and consumer spending to forecast trends, though predictions are rarely perfect. Understanding basic economics helps citizens make better decisions about saving, investing, and voting.
