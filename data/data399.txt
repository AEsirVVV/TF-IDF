Field Notes: Ink Drawing
The argument about tonal never ends, so I test both and compare numbers. Beginners overlook fixative and then struggle with tonal again and again. The routine splits into three parts: preparing crosshatch, working contour, finishing fixative.
The toolkit can stay small; fixative and crosshatch are enough if the technique is right. If only one habit sticks, let it be this: keep tonal slow and fixative even. Over a long stretch nib makes more difference than any quick trick.
Another note: fixative varies far more than tonal in this batch.
